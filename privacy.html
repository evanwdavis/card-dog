Privacy Policy

Last updated June 16, 2026

Card Dog does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Ever.

Overview

Card Dog is a memory card game for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. We built it to be simple, fun, and completely private. The app has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising.

Data we collect

None. Card Dog does not collect any information about you or your device. Your game progress and personal bests are stored locally on your device only and are never transmitted anywhere.

Third-party services

Card Dog does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. No data is shared with any third party.

Children's privacy

Card Dog is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no data whatsoever, there are no special considerations for children's privacy — nothing is collected from anyone, regardless of age.

Apple platforms

Card Dog is distributed through the Apple App Store. Apple may collect certain information as part of the App Store experience, subject to Apple's Privacy Policy. We have no access to or control over any information Apple collects.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. We don't anticipate changes — our commitment to collecting no data is fundamental to how Card Dog works.
